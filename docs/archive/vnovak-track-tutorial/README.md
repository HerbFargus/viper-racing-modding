into the game, recovered from the Internet Archive and kept here verbatim.

## Why this is in the repo

The pages lived at `vnovak.com/vrtracktogametutpart1.html` through `part4`, and
the Bob's Track Builder one at
`vnovak.com/vr/btb2racer-2viper-racing-track-making-tutorial.html`. Captured
**13 February 2020**; three of the five have only that single capture.

Val Novak is not a marginal figure in this game's history. `vnovak.com` went up
in December 2003 and became the hub: the *Car Mods*, *Track Tools* and *Track
Mods* archives this repo's [modding history](../../history/modding-history.md)
cites were sourced from it, he built the **1.2.5 (2016)** and **1.2.6 (2017)**
community patches and the View Extender, and 1,668 of the 2,023 assets in the
[community gallery](https://herbfargus.github.io/viper-racing-gallery/) come
from his `valscars` and `valstracks` collections. These are the track-making
instructions from the person who made most of the tracks.

They also fill the gap the other archived tutorial leaves. [Wrxds's
tutorial](../wrxds-car-tutorial/) covers building a **car**; this covers a
**track**, which is the harder half and the one this project's own
[`trackgen`](../../reference/file-formats.md) work had to reconstruct from the
binaries rather than from anyone's notes.

## What was changed

The pages are as published, with three mechanical edits:

- the Internet Archive's toolbar and scripts removed;
- every image rewritten to `images/` and **all 105 downloaded**, so the pages
  work from disk with no network;
- a banner added at the top of each page saying what it is, so a local copy is
  never mistaken for the live site.

Links to *other* sites — ZModeler, Bob's Track Builder, XVI32, Paint Shop Pro —
are left pointing at their Internet Archive captures rather than at hosts that
may be gone. Links to the rest of `vnovak.com` are left pointing at the archive
too, since only these pages are copied here.

`track2gamefiles.rar` is Val's own template kit, fetched from
`vnovak.com/tools/`: `.mod` templates for signs and buildings, `.tga` textures,
`aidef.ccs` and `camera.tab`, dated between 2007 and 2015. The tutorial is close
to unusable without it, which is why it is here rather than linked.

## Provenance and rights

This is preservation, not authorship. The tutorials and the template kit are Val
Novak's work, reproduced so that they do not disappear with the host. If you are
the author and would rather they were not here, open an issue and they will be
removed.

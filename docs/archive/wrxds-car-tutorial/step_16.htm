Step 16: Finalizing the Car

This is the time to do any final adjustments. If you notice something that you find annoying for the 50th test drive, this is the time to fix it.

I noticed the dashboard needles were just a little off, so I fixed them.

The brake lights were just a little too far on the outside, so I opened ZModeler and re-aligned them too.

Also, I checked up on Adobe's web site, and they have a plug-in update for the Targa format, so you can save your precious alpha channels again!

Once you are ready for this, you can move ONWARD.

Pet Obstacle Mockups

Six low-poly cats and dogs for obj obstacle ball lines. Each model is centred on its own middle, so the axes marker is where the game puts the ball's centre.

Flat-shaded boxes and wedges, one colour per face, so they suit a palette or a small atlas.

Dogs vs Cats

A layout mockup for the new track. A fence runs down the middle. The lap crosses into dog country at the start gate and comes back into cat country through a giant cat flap. Everything is built at pet scale, 4× life size.

The lap, in order

    Pets on the lap

    Every pet is a 50 lb obstacle ball at 4× size. Dogs stay on the dog side, cats on the cat side.

    Open questions

    • Which track slot this replaces, and whether it runs with AI or solo only. Spawned obstacles plus AI crashed in Ball Pit; this is the first test to run.
    • Hydrants and the scratching post are fixed (static) collision in the .sol, not obstacles.
    • The rooftop drop and frisbee gap need tuning in game. The heights are first guesses.
    • The tennis balls could also be 50 lb obstacles. They're scenery for now.
